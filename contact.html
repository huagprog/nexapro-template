Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions we receive from our clients.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A standard website takes 2-4 weeks, while complex projects can take 8-12 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during consultation.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes! All our plans include post-launch support. The duration varies by plan (30-365 days), and we offer extended maintenance packages for ongoing needs.

Can I customize the template after purchase?

Absolutely! The template is built with CSS variables, making it easy to customize colors, fonts, and styles. All code is well-commented for easy modification.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all digital products. If you're not satisfied, contact us within 14 days for a full refund, no questions asked.
